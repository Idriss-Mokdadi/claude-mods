Crab themes: animated preview

Each strip is the left sidebar. All seven play the same scenario so you can compare. No context meter. Jellyfish = subagents (one per agent). Water/sky bands = 5-hour limit, filling part by part. 10 small items on the ground = 7-day limit. Light (day → night) and sleeping crabs = cache.

TasksA building grows one level per step; finished = flag + celebration
